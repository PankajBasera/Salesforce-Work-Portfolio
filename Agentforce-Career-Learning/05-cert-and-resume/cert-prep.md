## High-yield reminders

- Agents assist; **permissions and actions** define what can actually happen
- Grounding quality = corpus + chunking + retriever + refuse-when-empty
- Prompt templates are **platform artifacts** (access, activate, execute)
- Testing Center is how you argue production readiness
- Multi-agent: split on **trust / corpus / ownership**, not fashion
