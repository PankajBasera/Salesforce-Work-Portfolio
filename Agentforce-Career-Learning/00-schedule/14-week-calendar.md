## Cadence reminder

- **Do not rush weeks 9–12** (Data 360 + lifecycle are the usual failure mode).
- Skim pure “what is an LLM” units; **never skip** Prompt, Trust Layer, Agentforce Builder, Testing Center.
- Every Trailhead hands-on → do it in a real org the same day.
